    The PNG copies with a transparent background, so it sits on a page without a white box.

    Reading a diagram. Strings run G–C–E–A left to right. The thick bar at the top is the nut; 5fr at the right means the window starts at the 5th fret instead.
    Barres. A long rounded bar means one finger lies flat across those strings.
    Naming. Each diagram is labelled with how the chord was understood — so C#M coming back as C♯ rather than C♯m is visible before you paste.
