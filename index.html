Guitar and Ukulele Chord Strip

Add professional chord diagrams to your PDF sheet music and create formatted chord charts

Launch App → Read Manual
📄

PDF Integration

Upload PDF sheet music and overlay chord diagrams directly. Position and resize with intuitive drag-and-drop controls.

🎯

Chord Recognition

Automatically detects chord names from text or PDFs. Handles variations from simple (C, Cm) to complex (Cmaj7, Em7b5).

🎸

Dual Instruments

Full support for guitar and ukulele with standard tunings. Accurate fingering diagrams for all chords.

🎶

Strumming Patterns

Browse 50+ authentic strumming patterns, each with song examples. Perfect for learning and reference.

📝

Sheet Building

Create formatted PDFs from plain text lyrics. Automatic reflowing with customizable margins and styling.

🌙

Dark/Light Modes

Comfortable viewing in any lighting. Automatic theme based on system preferences with manual override.
